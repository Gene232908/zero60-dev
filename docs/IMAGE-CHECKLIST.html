Two rules when exporting from Canva
1. Use the export size shown, or larger — never smaller. These are already 2× so images stay sharp on phones and retina screens. Smaller will look soft, which is exactly the problem the current photos have.
2. Match the ratio. The layout crops to the ratio shown, from the centre. Send a square image for a 21:9 slot and the top and bottom of your subject get cut off. Colour grading does not matter much — several slots are converted to black & white automatically to match the brand.
