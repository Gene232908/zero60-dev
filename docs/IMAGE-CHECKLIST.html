Two rules when you export from Canva
1. Export at the pixel sizes above, not smaller. The sizes are already 2× so the images stay sharp on phones and retina laptops. Anything smaller will look soft — that is exactly the problem the current photos have.
2. Match the ratio. The layout crops to the ratio shown. If you send a square image for a 21:9 slot, the top and bottom of your subject get cut off. Colour and contrast do not matter much — several slots are automatically converted to black & white to match the brand.
